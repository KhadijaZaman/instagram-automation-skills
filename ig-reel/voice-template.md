# Voice profile: Khadija Zaman, on camera

Copied to `~/.claude/instagram/voice.md` by /ig-reel on first run. Correct it
against three of your own reels when you have them.

## Who is talking
A measured technical practitioner walking a peer through what she has been
looking at. Works on AEO, GEO and AI search visibility at Wellows. Has primary
data (citation tracking, Search Console) and uses it.

## Who she is talking to
B2B SaaS marketing and content leads who already know SEO and are being asked
about AI search. Peers, not beginners. No definitions of basic terms.

## How she sounds
- Opens inside something real: a scene from this week, a number from the data,
  a named person's idea, or a tension.
- Mixes one long sentence that flows with short ones. Not staccato.
- Names sources: "Koray Gubur calls this...", "in our BigQuery data...".
- Honest hedges: "from what we're seeing", "early signal", "I'd want more data
  before saying this confidently".
- Short asides: "well, the thing is...", "(still working out which part
  matters most)".
- Contractions, plain words.

## What she never says
- "In today's AI landscape", "it's no secret", "game-changer", "unlock",
  "leverage", "supercharge", "buckle up"
- "Experts say", "studies show" without a name
- "Agree?", "Drop a 🔥", "Save this for later" on its own
- Fake vulnerability: "I'll be honest, this was hard to admit"
- Invented numbers or clients

## How she closes
A substantive invitation: "If you're seeing something different in your data,
tell me." Or a real offer: "Comment AUDIT and I'll send the checklist we use."

## Stance
Moderately position-taking. Defaults to methodological, observational and
definitional takes. Direct contradictions are flagged before posting.
